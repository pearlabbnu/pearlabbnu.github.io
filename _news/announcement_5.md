🎉 **New Publication in the Journal of Adolescent Health!**

Congratulations to **Guanheng Ren** and **Zhiqian Zhang** on their co-first-authored paper published in the *Journal of Adolescent Health*! The study, titled "[Early Pubertal Timing and Depressive Symptoms in Boys and Girls: The Moderating Role of Parental Pubertal Support](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1054139X26003642)," highlights the protective role of parental support in adolescent mental health during puberty.
