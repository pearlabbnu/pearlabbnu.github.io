🏆 **PEAR Lab Students Present Research at a National Conference!**

PEAR Lab student **Jiayi Yang** presented her research at the **18th Annual Academic Conference of the Developmental Psychology Committee of the Chinese Psychological Society** in Chengdu, Sichuan. She delivered an oral presentation on pubertal emotions and adolescent mental health and received the **"Rising Star" Award** at the Graduate Student Forum. Congratulations to Jiayi on this well-deserved recognition!
